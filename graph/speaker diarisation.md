Partitioning a stream of human speech by identity of speaker.

Why it matters for meeting evidence: a transcript without speakers (single microphone, "Microphone / System audio") cannot tell who committed to an action or who holds a document. Before acting on a recollection of who said what, read the diarised transcript (e.g. the one recorded by the meeting agent, which keeps each participant's name). The speaker label is the [[Provenance]] of a spoken statement.
