proposed explanation for an observation, phenomenon, or scientific [[problem]] (Wikidata).

In a qualitative market study, hypotheses come first from the team's intuitions and early analyses and are kept in a **hypothesis register**: one row per hypothesis with its sources ([[documentary evidence]], interviews), its strength, and what would disprove it ([[falsifiability]]). Interviews and coded documents then move each row up or down ([[belief revision]]).

Discipline in interviews: start from concrete cases before showing a hypothesis to the respondent, otherwise the answer confirms the question (see [[critical incident technique]], [[member check]]).
