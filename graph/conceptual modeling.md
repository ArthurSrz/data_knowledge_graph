The activity of building a [[conceptual model]]: naming the concepts of a domain and the relations between them, so people (and agents) share one representation of it. An [[Ontology]], a [[knowledge graph]] or the mental map of a knowledge store are its outputs.

The activity is an occurrent; the [[conceptual model]] it produces is not. Hence `product of`, not `broader`, between the two.
