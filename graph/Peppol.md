European standard and network for exchanging electronic invoices.

With French e-invoicing becoming mandatory, every billed entity needs a SIREN/SIRET, a VAT number, a Peppol routing ID and a reception channel (PDP, the accredited e-invoicing platform). These become required columns of a billing file.
