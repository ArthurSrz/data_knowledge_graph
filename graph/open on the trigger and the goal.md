State, before anything else and in the [[brief]], why the mapping happens now (the trigger) and what it will make possible (« at the end of this mapping we will be able to… », followed by two or three concrete uses). Every later output (scope, interview questions, deck, demo) opens with these two lines, and figures come only after them.

Condition: a trigger exists and someone owns the goal; a mapping with neither is documentation for its own sake.

Limitation: a benchmark gap shown without its trigger reads as noise; and the goal fixed at kick-off can shift, so the brief must be revisited when it does.
