A function, sometimes assumed to preserve structure: it associates each element of one set to exactly one element of another (left-total + right-unique). [[bijectivity]] is the special case where the mapping is one-to-one and onto.

Mathematical sense only — the business sense ("mapping a [[process]]") lives in [[business process mapping]].
