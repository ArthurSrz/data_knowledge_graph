Write the method as a branded document of two pages at most, one block per step (set the context, manage the guides, prepare an interview, during and after). Each step gives its rule in a sentence, a link to the screen or [[artifact]] it names, and the exact prompt to paste into the assistant. An artifact the method relies on but that does not exist yet is marked as « to create » and points to the place it will live. The cover message is drafted for the sender, informal, and sent by them with the document attached.

Condition: the steps were shown in a working session first; the document recalls, it does not teach from scratch.

Limitation: every link fails for a reader who has no access to the workspace; check membership of every recipient before sending, or the method is unreadable where it matters most.
