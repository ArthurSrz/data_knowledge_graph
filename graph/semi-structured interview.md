social science interview method.

[[Clarifeye]]'s interviews are semi-structured: a draft fixes topics and questions, and the coach follows up within them (topics to review, topic [[skills]] such as [[checklist]] or use-case exploration). Drafts are rehearsed with [[test]] runs before being sent.
