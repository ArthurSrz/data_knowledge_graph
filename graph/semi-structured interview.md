social science interview method.

[[Clarifeye]]'s interviews are semi-structured: a draft (`draft_interviews`) fixes topics and questions, and the coach follows up within them (`topics_to_review`, topic [[skills]] such as [[checklist]] or use_case_exploration). Drafts are rehearsed with [[test]] runs before being sent.
