**Semi-structured interview**: social science interview method.

Clarifeye's interviews are semi-structured: a draft (`draft_interviews`) fixes topics and questions, and the coach follows up within them (`topics_to_review`, topic skills such as checklist or use_case_exploration). Drafts are rehearsed with test runs before being sent; in the mapping build, about 60 test runs and 2 completed interviews.
