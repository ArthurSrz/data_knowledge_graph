**Sampling bias**: bias in which a sample is collected in such a way that some members of the intended population are less likely to be included than others (Wikidata).

In an interview campaign, the sample is the respondents: qualify each one first (role, size and type of the accounts they've handled) so you can see which parts of the population nobody speaks for.
