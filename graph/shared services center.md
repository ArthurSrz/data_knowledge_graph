Organization for sharing services between a variety of clients.

Mapping pitfall: a shared center executes, in whole or in part, processes that belong to the countries it serves. "Center procedures" are then country processes run by the center, not the center's own processes. Which accounts go to the center and which stay local is often an unwritten routing rule, to capture explicitly.
