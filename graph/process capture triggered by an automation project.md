Sub-situation of [[process capture for a BPM repository]]: the mapping is not an end in itself but is launched because something is about to change: an AI agent will take over part of the process, local rules must converge, or a [[benchmarking]] shows a performance gap.

Constraints specific to it:
- The [[trigger]] (why now) and the [[goal]] (« at the end of this mapping we will be able to… ») must be stated first, in the [[brief]]; they decide how deep the capture goes.
- Figures mean nothing to the audience until they are tied to the trigger: open with the trigger and the goal, then show the numbers.
- The capture must reach the level where steps can be assigned to an agent or to a person; above it, the map cannot support the decision.
- The gap the benchmark reveals becomes a [[gap analysis]] question for the experts, not a conclusion.
