**Audit trail**: chronological record of a sequence of activities, kept for security purposes.

The store's [[changelog]] (who, when, artifact version, conversation) works as an audit trail of what agents and users changed. It complements [[provenance]], which records where a *statement* came from, not who edited it. Both are needed to rebuild a session ([[agent observability]]).
