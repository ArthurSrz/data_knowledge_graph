concerns what is, was or will be, and expresses no approval or disapproval of what should be.

In [[process]] capture, it is AS-IS [[evidence]]: "publication after approval is automatic", "nothing blocks submission today". The discipline is that a positive statement about the configuration **does not close** a governance question (a [[normative statement]]): "nothing blocks this" says there's no configuration blocker, not that governance is settled.
