**Positive statement**: concerns what is, was or will be, and expresses no approval or disapproval of what should be.

In process capture, it is AS-IS evidence: "publication after approval is automatic", "no submission blocking today". Clara's discipline is that a positive statement about the configuration **does not close** a governance question (a [[normative statement]]): "nothing blocks the first capture" says there's no configuration blocker, not that governance is settled.
