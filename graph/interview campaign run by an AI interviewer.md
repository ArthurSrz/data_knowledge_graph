Sub-situation of [[tacit knowledge spread across expert profiles]]: a client team runs a series of interviews (a handful per profile) and delegates the interviewing to an AI agent that joins the video call.

Constraints specific to it:
- The agent only knows what its [[brief]] says: an interview whose brief was never validated runs without it.
- The agent is a guest in someone else's call: the host can remove it, and the interview is then lost.
- Each task (guide, brief, analysis) lives in its own conversation, so the [[interview guide]] and per-interview results must be kept as [[artifact]]s to carry over.
- The client team learns the tool while running the campaign.
