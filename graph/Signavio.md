Target of an AS-IS capture: Clara produces BPMN 2.0 XML, the BPO imports it into a WIP process map (local models go in their own directories), enriches it and submits it through *Share > Start approval workflow*.

Its **Dictionary** is the [[controlled vocabulary]] for roles, organisations, systems, data objects and KPIs. Missing entries go through *sandbox* categories pending review. IT systems are not created in Signavio: they come from the architecture repository through an interface.

Current configuration (interview, 2026-11): approvers assigned automatically from a business-defined matrix; automatic publication after approval; no submission blocking; Variant Management not used; access managed manually, with an access-management tool planned.
