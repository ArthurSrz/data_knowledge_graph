After a working session, a client team must use a tool on its own: the vendor promised a written method, and the team will read it between other tasks.

Constraints: the document is read once, fast, and must fit in very little space (two pages); every screen or [[artifact]] it names should be one click away; instructions to an AI assistant work best as ready-to-paste prompts rather than descriptions; an artifact the method mentions but that does not exist yet must be marked as such, not linked as if it did.
