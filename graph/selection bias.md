**Selection bias**: selection of individuals, groups or data for analysis such that proper randomization is not achieved (Wikidata).

Example: a corpus of calls for tenders over-represents buyers who are required to publish them (public sector), so it can't stand alone for a market. Narrower: [[sampling bias]].
