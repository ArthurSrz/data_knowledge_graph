In BFO, an entity that unfolds itself in time: it has temporal parts and happens, rather than exists. [[Process]], [[occurrence]] and events are occurrents.

Disjoint with [[continuant]]: nothing can both happen and persist through time keeping its identity. The reasoner flags any concept that falls under both.
