Out of every open point, keep about six whose answer changes the model and that someone in the room can answer. Give each one slide: the question, what each source says (cited), and two or three mutually exclusive options with their impact on the model, the current model's option marked. Name who answers the deferred points. Record each decision with its reason and source right after the meeting, and change a model only if the flow changes.

Condition: the points are already classified as contradiction (two facts disagree), difference (rule versus practice) or gap (nothing documented).

Limitation: gaps cannot be settled in the room; they leave as named questions for arbitration interviews.
