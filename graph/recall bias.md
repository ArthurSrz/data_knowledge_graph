**Recall bias**: a [[cognitive bias]] in which what a person remembers of past events is distorted, in accuracy or completeness (Wikidata: "type of cognitive bias").

In interviews about past deals or projects, it's why asking for one specific recent case ([[critical incident technique]]) works better than asking for a generalization.
