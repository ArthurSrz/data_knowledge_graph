Shortened form of a word or phrase in writing.

Risk in source citations: a legend that defines a short form ("Tool = training module") makes every "Tool p.X" a citation of that document, while the same short form elsewhere may name another document or the tool itself. Write the document's identifier in full when two documents could share the short form. See [[Provenance]].
