**Triangulation**: results-testing methodology (Wikidata) that checks a finding against several independent sources, methods or observers (Denzin).

In a market study: tenders, churn analyses, client workshop recordings, an internal synthesis, buyers' reference frameworks and interviews each carry a different [[bias]]. A [[hypothesis]] becomes strong when sources with *different* biases agree, not when many sources sharing one bias repeat it. Weight each source by how representative it is and what it adds.
