Process of pursuing payments of debts owed by individuals or businesses.

Typical as-is structure met in a process capture: prioritise accounts, contact the customer, then branch on the answer (payment promise, dispute, payment plan, no answer), monitor until payment is evidenced, otherwise escalate (account blocking, legal recovery). The unit of work is usually the customer account, not the invoice. Recurring frictions: the dispute handover to the team that resolves it, who follows the balance afterwards, and who approves payment plans.
