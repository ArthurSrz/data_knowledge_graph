**Tacit knowledge**: knowledge that is hard to transfer by writing it down or saying it (Polanyi, 1958).

It surfaces as a user correcting an agent ("match on this identifier, not that one", "these two codes are not the same thing"). A knowledge store's job is to turn it into [[explicit knowledge]]: a rule, a [[checklist]], a class in the mental map.

The conversion keeps its [[provenance]]: the rule is written impersonally, and the link points back to the interview turn it came from. See [[explicit knowledge]] for the three grades (attributed position → current-state fact → adopted rule).

See [[SECI model of knowledge dimensions]] (externalization).
