**Change control**: process that brings changes to a product or system in a controlled, coordinated way, avoiding unplanned changes and the faults they introduce.

How I run Clara: Clara **proposes** numbered edits, each with its supporting evidence; I **approve one at a time** ("start with the 6th point", "go on with Edit 1"); Clara applies it as a [[changeset]] and reports what changed. When an applied change crosses a boundary, I use a [[manual override]]. Every applied change lands in the store's [[changelog]], which works as the [[audit trail]].
