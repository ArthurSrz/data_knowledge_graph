**Knowledge management**: the process of creating, sharing, using and managing an organization's knowledge.

At Clarifeye (doctrine "Structuring Collective Know-How"), the *Knowledge Manager* runs the loop: Scoping, then repeated rounds of Identify → Capture → Reconcile → Use.

Lesson from Deepki billing (Nov. 2026): when an agent fails in use, it is usually the store that's missing something, not the model. 5 of the 8 problems a user ran into came from uncaptured [[tacit knowledge]]. The fix is to capture it in a [[checklist]] per customer, not to change the model.
