An intended result, with a criterion that tells when it is reached.

For a mapping, the goal is set at kick-off and written in the [[brief]] as « à l'issue de ce mapping, on pourra… » followed by concrete uses (which steps to hand to an AI agent, which local rules to converge). It decides how deep the capture goes; the [[trigger]] says why now.
