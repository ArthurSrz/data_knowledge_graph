practice of collecting the requirements of a system from users, customers and other stakeholders

In [[Clarifeye]], elicitation runs as short interviews with [[subject-matter expert]]s (one interviewee per interview). Each answer feeds the store as an attributed [[positive statement]] or position, linked to its turn for [[provenance]].
