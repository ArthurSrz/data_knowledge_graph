A colleague of the main user receives a link to a SaaS workspace and cannot open it. The team's first reflex is to go through IT, which can take days.

Constraints: the usual cause is simpler, the person was never invited to that workspace ([[access control]] by membership); whether IT must approve a new tool depends on the client's policy (SSO, procurement), which the vendor cannot see. The vendor may also lack the rights to check who is a member.
