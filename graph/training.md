Acquisition of [[knowledge]], [[skills]] and competencies as a result of teaching. Wikidata places it *part of* [[onboarding]].

Distinct from [[training data set]] / [[Training algorithm]], which belong to the ML sense of "training" (fitting a [[model]]), not to human [[learning]].

## Training material as a source

In a process capture, training documents (tool modules, onboarding decks for new joiners) describe the process as taught, which can lag or idealise current practice. Keep them separated from the procedures, in their own collection, and use them only as leads; see [[Provenance]]. They are still worth reading: the gap between what is taught and what is done is often a friction worth recording.
