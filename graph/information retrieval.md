**Information retrieval**: activity of obtaining information resources relevant to an information need from a collection of information resources.

In a Clarifeye store, retrieval runs over the Library's chunks through tools: `get-chunk` (204 calls in the mapping build), `document-outline` (75), `store_search` (67), plus `agentic_search` grading (3,297 calls). [[RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)]] is retrieval feeding an LLM's answer.
