A person with special knowledge, skills or authority in a particular area. In a process capture, the SMEs are the Contributors: they answer short interviews, share documents and correct drafts; the [[process owner]] arbitrates between them.

Related: [[elicitation interview]].

## A departing SME

When an SME is leaving, order the work by dependency on them: first whatever only they can do (comment the drafts, hand over the documents they hold, name who knows what), then the internal modelling work. Ask for comments directly in the artifacts rather than through the agent, so the feedback is attributed and traceable.

Before acting on a recollection of what the SME said ("they said someone else has those files"), read the speaker-attributed transcript: a notes app's transcript may lack speakers, the meeting agent's transcript has them. The two can disagree on who committed to what.
