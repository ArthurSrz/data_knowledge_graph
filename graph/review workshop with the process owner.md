Sub-situation of [[process capture for a BPM repository]]: after a first capture round (sources loaded, one or two interviews, first models and registers drafted), the consultant has about one hour with the [[process owner]] and the experts already interviewed. It is the collective form of a [[member check]].

Constraints specific to it:
- Only a handful of points (about six) fit in the hour: keep those whose answer changes the model (flow, lanes, gateways, end events, responsibilities) and that someone in the room can answer; name who answers the rest.
- Each point shows what the sources say, cited, and two or three mutually exclusive options with their impact; the option the current model implements is marked.
- Contradiction, difference and gap must be told apart: two facts that disagree, a rule versus practice, nothing documented.
- The deck uses the participants' words and the organisation's own template; preparation must take few exchanges, or the consultant will not use the tool.
- The compliance [[audit]] of the models comes after the workshop, once the decisions are recorded, and before the owner's validation.
