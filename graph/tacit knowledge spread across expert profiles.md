What an organisation knows about a domain sits in the heads of several [[subject-matter expert]]s, and each profile has seen a different part of it (e.g. people who win new accounts vs. people who keep existing ones). Nothing written covers the whole.

Constraints: no single interview gives the full picture; each statement is only valid from where its author stands, so its [[Provenance]] must be kept; experts have little time.
