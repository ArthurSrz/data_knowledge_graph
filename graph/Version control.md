**Version control**: activity of managing the versions of one or more files.

Every write to a Clarifeye artifact creates an `ArtifactVersion` (version_number, change_summary, edit_source) and a [[changelog]] entry; the mapping store reached 737 entries between 3 and 20 Nov 2026, 569 of them written by Clara.
