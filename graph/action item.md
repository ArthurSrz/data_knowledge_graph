Management term regarding a documented event, task, activity, or action that needs to take place.

Written as the need, with its owner when known and the meeting it comes from ("interlocutor to identify: …"), not as an account of what was done. When an action is superseded (e.g. a review goes to another person), strike it and say what replaced it rather than deleting it. See [[project plan]].
