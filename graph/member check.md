**Member check**: returning data or results to participants to check for accuracy (Wikidata).

Placed at the end of an interview, after the open part, so showing the interim results doesn't steer the answers. It's the respondent-side twin of the reviewer step in [[semi-structured interview]], and a route from grade 1 to grade 2 in [[explicit knowledge]] — though for grade 2 the checker must be someone other than the source.
