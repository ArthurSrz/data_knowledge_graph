esearch technique (Wikidata) in which a researcher gathers [[data]] by questioning a respondent. Narrower: [[semi-structured interview]].

Three moves from a market-study campaign:
1. **Qualify the respondent first** (role, how many and what kind of accounts they handled), to read each answer against its position ([[sampling bias]]).
2. **Cases before hypotheses**: ask about concrete situations ([[critical incident technique]]) before showing any [[hypothesis]], to avoid [[cognitive bias]] from the question itself.
3. **Provisional results last**: only in a second part, show the interim outputs and let the respondent react ([[member check]]).

One common guide, plus questions specific to each profile.
