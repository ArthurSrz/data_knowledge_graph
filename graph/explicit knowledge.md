**Explicit knowledge**: knowledge that has been articulated, codified and stored in some medium.

In a knowledge store it comes in grades that Clara keeps separate:
1. **attributed position**: one person's view, kept with its [[provenance]] (Julien: "Local BPO is accountable");
2. **current-state fact**: a [[positive statement]] about how things are configured today (approvers assigned automatically);
3. **[[business rule]]**: a [[normative statement]] adopted by whoever owns the decision (the [[process owner]], or the user closing a gap).

Moving from one grade to the next takes a decision. Rephrasing doesn't do it.
