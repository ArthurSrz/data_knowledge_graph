The ongoing [[process]] of gathering, evaluating and updating what a [[knowledge base]] holds about a topic, for as long as the topic matters.

In [[clarifeye]] it is the core loop of a knowledge store: collect (documents, interviews, meetings), evaluate (signals and [[feedback]] from users), update the [[artifact]]s. A store that stops curating drifts from practice.
