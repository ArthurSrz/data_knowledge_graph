Diagram in flowcharts: a band that groups the steps performed by one role or organisational unit.

In [[Business Process Model and Notation]] a lane partitions a pool by role, and every flow node sits in exactly one lane. A lane named "Role A or Role B" hides a routing decision; when the two roles must be shown, the lane is split and a gateway routes the work (see the BPMN note, "Splitting a shared role lane"). Each lane should match a role column of the [[responsibility assignment matrix]].
