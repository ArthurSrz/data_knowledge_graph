a set of procedures used for collecting direct [[observations]] of human behavior that have critical significance and meet methodically defined criteria (Wikidata; Flanagan, 1954).

In interviews: "tell me about the last deal you lost to this kind of client" instead of "what do these clients need?". A specific incident limits [[recall bias]] and keeps the respondent from echoing the team's [[hypothesis]].
