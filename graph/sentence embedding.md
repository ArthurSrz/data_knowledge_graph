**Sentence embedding**: numeric representation of a sentence.

What makes a chunk findable by meaning rather than by keyword in a store's Library: chunks are embedded at indexing (`ExtractionFlow`) and queries are matched against them. The sentence-level counterpart of [[Word embeddings]].
