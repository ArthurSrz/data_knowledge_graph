A versioned work product that outlives the conversation or task that produced it: a brief, an interview guide, a playbook, a summary table. It is an [[information content entity]]; producing or updating it is a process, which [[artifact management]] covers.

In [[Clarifeye]], artifacts are what link conversations together: **one conversation = one task**, and what one task produces (the store brief, an [[interview guide]], a per-interview sheet) is written into an artifact that the next conversation reads. Without it, each conversation starts from zero. An artifact that is mentioned but not yet created is a gap to fill, not a link to fake.
