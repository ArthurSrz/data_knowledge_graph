**Checklist**: a job aid for repetitive tasks or procedures.

Pattern seen in a Clarifeye knowledge store (Deepki billing, Nov. 2026): one checklist per customer and per cycle, in two parts.
- **Client file** that persists across cycles ([[master data]]: payers, billing level, contract terms).
- **Per-cycle execution** (12 steps, status, evidence, blockers) that marks the [[Human-in-the-loop]] checkpoints.

It is where the customer-specific [[tacit knowledge]] that would otherwise get lost in chat corrections gets written down.
