**Emic and etic**: two kinds of linguistic field research (Wikidata; Pike, 1954). *Emic* describes a group in its own categories and words; *etic* describes it in the observer's categories.

Learning a market's vocabulary is emic work: how the customers name their problems. The vendor's product terms are etic. In a [[controlled vocabulary]], emic terms enter as [[non-preferred term]]s mapped to the [[preferred term]], so a search in the customer's words still finds the vendor's concept.
