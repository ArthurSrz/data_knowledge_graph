**Prompt engineering**: creation or optimization of a prompt to be given to an artificial intelligence model.

In Clarifeye it is packaged rather than typed each time: skills loaded with `get_skill` (map_process, fill_artifacts, create_artifacts, find_gaps, the custom `al-map-process`), slash commands, and artifact definitions that Clara read 212 times before writing. Choosing what goes into the window around the prompt is [[context engineering]].
