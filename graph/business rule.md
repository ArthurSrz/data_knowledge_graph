**Business rule**: rule that defines or constrains an aspect of the business, applying to people, processes, corporate behaviour or computing systems.

[[Business Process Model and Notation]] *depicts* business rules, but many never reach the diagram: they live in the store's mapping rules, e.g. "new vocabulary entries are reviewed manually, outside the tool's workflow", "missing IT systems are created in the system of record, then synchronised into the BPM tool".

A business rule is a [[normative statement]] once adopted. Before that, a rule-shaped sentence taken from an interview is only a [[positive statement]] or an attributed position, and its [[provenance]] must say so.
