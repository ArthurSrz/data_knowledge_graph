An [[artifact]] stating what a piece of work is for, for whom, and within which scope.

In a Clarifeye knowledge store it is the only mandatory artifact. In a [[business process mapping]] store it carries the [[trigger]] (why now) and the [[goal]] (« à l'issue de ce mapping, on pourra… »), which together decide how deep the capture goes.
