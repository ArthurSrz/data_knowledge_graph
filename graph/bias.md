partiality that prevents objective or alternative consideration of an issue or [[situation]]

Every [[source of information]] has its own. Comparing sources means naming each one's bias, not looking for an unbiased one: tenders over-represent the buyers who must publish them ([[selection bias]]), an internal synthesis leans toward its author ([[conflict of interest]]), and a salesperson's account of past deals is reconstructed from [[memory]] ([[recall bias]]). [[Triangulation (social science)]] weighs them against each other.
