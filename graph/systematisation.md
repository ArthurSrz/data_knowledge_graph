**Systematisation**: process of integrating disparate knowledge into a unified system.

What a Clarifeye curation session does: several interviews ([[requirements elicitation]]) and documents are folded into one method, one register and one set of mapping rules, each kept as a [[single source of truth]]. The combination step of the [[SECI model of knowledge dimensions]], carried out with [[information integration]] and governed by [[belief revision]].
