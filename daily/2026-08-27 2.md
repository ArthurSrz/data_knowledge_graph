How to turn super-powerful AI models into massive time and cost savings inside organisations ? 

The simple answer is: catch people before they jump to their [[Claude]] and incentivize them to sit and write down what they know. 

We talked to a business legal team that did the same complex document analysis **without AI**; **with AI ([[Claude]])**; with a 1h unwritten [[knowledge]] capture [[session]] + AI. They told us the time it took to complete the tasks and the AI bill they received afterwards : 

* Without AI : 4 days to complete the tasks
* With AI : 1 day and 500€ worth of tokens 
* With AI + [[knowledge]] capture [[session]]: 1 hour and 44€ worth of tokens. 

We then took a look at their conversation. We discovered that what saved them time and €€ was that with [[knowledge]] being captured, the AI stopped re-reading the same hundred pages of document over and over again. It read it once and single-shot the right analysis. Also, with [[knowledge]] captured, the number of iterations between [[collaborators]] fell down to 0 as AI could leverage the [[knowledge]] poured into the system by senior members reviewing the analysis.
