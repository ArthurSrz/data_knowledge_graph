At [[Clarifeye]], we believe that the most useful pieces of information lie in people' heads. 

They are most crucial at 4 specific moments  : 
1. when conducting AI transformation projects : without people's [[knowledge]] captured and added to agents' instruction, they will fail 90 % of the time
2. during any IT migration endeavor : how legacy systems really worked and how the [[target]] one should is rarely written down
3. to support [[process]] improvement and operational excellence : only people know what are the [[best]] recipes for their business 
4. when people are leaving the company : they usually walk always with strategic [[knowledge]] and not capturing it is always a loss 

So we made [[Clarifeye]] as a company [[knowledge]] central station. It can read company documents, email exchanges, GenAI conversations and organize interview campaigns inside your organization. 

This way you can keep your [[process]] maps, business [[rules]] and playbooks up to date and nurture any AI agents with the right [[context]].
